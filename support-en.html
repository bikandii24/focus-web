FAQ

What's a clean session?
One you finish without leaving the app (or that you marked “open” before starting because you needed other apps).

I left for a call and the session broke.
In Account → Session you can pick the mode: gentle, balanced or strict. Your first session is always balanced. With app blocking on (Focus Pro), you can leave the app without breaking the session.

How do I surf an urge without opening the app?
Lock Screen → long-press → Customize → add the “Focus: Surf an urge” control. Or Settings → Action Button → Controls. Or tell Siri “I have an urge in Focus”.

How does the night block work?
In the Sleep tab. At your bedtime, iOS covers the apps you pick until you get up, even with Focus closed. Flexible (free): after a guided minute you get 5 minutes. Strict (Pro): it can't be turned off until morning.

What's discreet mode?
Shake your phone: your urges get renamed “Habit 17”, “Habit 42”… Shake again and they're back.

Where's my data?
Only on your iPhone. Export and delete: Account → Your data. Privacy policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Focus. You can cancel anytime.
