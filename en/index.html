How it works

Friction, not willpower

Sessions that are hard to quit

Giving up takes up to 12 seconds of holding, with your phone buzzing and your own intention on screen. Leaving the app gets noticed.

I have the urge

60 guided seconds straight from your Lock Screen, without opening anything. The urge drops on its own if you give it time.

Real app blocking

During a session, iOS covers the apps you pick. You can leave Focus: they stay covered. The block comes off by itself at the end.

Night block

At your bedtime, your apps stay covered until you get up, even with Focus closed. Free.

Questions

What people usually ask

Does it block my whole phone?

No. It only covers the apps you pick, and only during a session or the night block. Calls, maps and the camera stay there.

What if I really need another app?

You say so before you start and the session stays clean. The point is deciding it cold, not halfway through.

Does it work if I close Focus?

Yes. iOS applies the block, so it stays on with the app closed, and comes off by itself at the end.

Do I need an account?

There's no account and no server. Nothing to remember and nothing that can leak.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in your Apple ID settings. The 14-day trial isn't charged if you cancel before it ends.
